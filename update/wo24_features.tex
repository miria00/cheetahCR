\paragraph{Hardware--software interaction.}
Hardware and software gains are not independent. The two must therefore be co-selected rather than
optimized in sequence. Added on-chip delivery and SRAM are what let storage
fusion keep intermediates on chip and make a wider mapping menu usable at all.
A larger menu buys nothing while execution is service-bound. The two model
families pull the chip in different directions. DeepSeek-V3 gains mostly from
placement and residency and favors a deep memory hierarchy with capacity for
resident experts and KV. The bandwidth-bound dense Llamas favor off-chip and
on-chip delivery and gain least from added arrays, and as their parameter
count grows, weight and KV residency accounts for a growing share of the gain,
so larger models favor capacity over delivery.

design exercises.
