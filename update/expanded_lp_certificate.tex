\paragraph{Full-domain lower from compact price fits.}
The successful proof assigns free prices $\pi_i$ to original exact-cover
equalities. For each admissible implementation $k$ with members $S_k$, its
member prices must be dominated by its actual priced resource cost. The source
compiler includes every genuine ancestral implementation and eligibility
condition; equal shapes do not imply equal availability. Disabled choices retain
their exact zero boxes. In the final resource construction on the 1,536 added
branches, let $H_b$ be the stored host rate, $B_b^{\rm cap}$ the stored bandwidth
cap, $D_k^{B}$ stream bytes, $H_k$ host bytes, $s_k$ paid host-setup ticks,
and $\mathcal I_k$ the original member set:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 a_b=\max\{1,256/H_b\},\qquad N_b=B_b^{\rm cap}+a_b H_b,\\
 C_{bk}=D_k^{B}+a_b(H_k+H_b s_k),\qquad
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal I_k}\pi_i\le C_{bk}.
 \end{gathered}
\]
The bandwidth-cap and host-service rows share the duration budget $\kappa$.
Their demand/work contributions cancel using actual implementation costs;
only cover-equality prices change. Each choice residual is
$\kappa(C_{bk}-\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_k}\pi_i)/N_b\ge0$.
Transfer residuals are nonnegative, unused unbounded-coordinate residuals
vanish, and the original box-support calculation supplies the complete lower.
All algebra uses the actual stored rational coefficients, including
$\kappa=4722366482869645/4722366482869645213696$.
Branch-specific normalizers and original-row multipliers are checked explicitly;
scalar gains from separate resource bounds are not simply added.
The earlier branches retain their own complete proofs. Zero-weight cases have
zero cover RHS, and the perspective weight residuals are $L_b-L\ge0$ for
$L=\min_bL_b$, certifying the entire family.

\paragraph{Execution remains a separate result.}

elsewhere in this paper. The new continuous certificate does not change any

execution establishes its membership, not an embedding theorem for every
possible execution. The LP upper is fractional and uses the frozen
reciprocal/product relaxation; neither it nor its small objective interval
proves near-optimality of the recovered chip.
