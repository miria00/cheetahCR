\section{Operator-Based Optimization with OCS}
\label{sec:solver_vm}\label{sec:ocs}\label{sec:vm_solver}\label{sec:mccormick_blowup}
The formulation combines independent choices with long storage trajectories and shared
hardware. Expanding every operation--event incidence is prohibitive. We adapt a variant of OCS
0.3.2 to solve the large scale problem.

\paragraph{Operators replace matrices.}
For a working LP $\min_x f(x)+g(Kx)$, an operator computes the forward action
$Kx$ and the adjoint $K^Ty$. Here $f(x)=c^Tx+I_{[\ell,u]}(x)$, and $g$
encodes row equalities and inequalities. The adjoint accumulates local
contributions to shared capacities and hardware. Generated operators avoid
storing the full modeling matrix, and does not require a factorization.

\paragraph{GPU first-order updates.}
The selected OCS path uses centered Halpern primal--dual hybrid gradient updates
with fixed diagonal steps and block-average restarts, building on first-order
splitting~\citep{chambolle2011first,halpern1967fixed,applegate2022practicallargescalelinearprogramming}.
Each update needs a forward action, an adjoint, and separable proximal operations.
Objective-shifted clipping for coordinate boxes, and RHS shifts plus sign
projection for inequality multipliers. JAX~\citep{jax2018github} compiles update blocks, while anchoring and restarts control iteration drift with an adaptive one-sided rescue controller.

\paragraph{Scaling and balanced steps.}
Reversible power-of-two row and coordinate scaling reduces coefficient disparity.
The adapter checks that unscaling recovers the original objective, rows and bounds.
Outward row/column sums provide diagonal steps $\Sigma,T$ satisfying
$\|\Sigma^{1/2}KT^{1/2}\|_2\le0.9$. A fixed reciprocal rescaling of the primal
and dual steps balances their progress while preserving that bound. Coefficient
range is a scaling diagnostic, not itself a condition number or a convergence
certificate.

\paragraph{Working sets and vector memory.}
GPU-resident supports use sparse or generated products. A separately tested
paged adapter keeps iteration vectors in file-backed arrays and transfers bounded
pages, accumulating global contributions before each proximal update. Paging
reduces GPU memory, not total vector storage, and incurs transfer overhead.
The expanded-residency working LP has $36{,}508$ columns and $313{,}061$ rows;
an A100 run reached a checked $0.0774\%$ working interval in $313.25$ seconds
including preparation and checking. 

\paragraph{Bounds, pricing, and design recovery.}
Floating iterates are proposals. Reconstruction checks primal rows and boxes
in original coordinates, legal dual signs and box support produce outward lower
bounds. A working lower does not cover omitted choices until pricing or a
complete-domain certificate accounts for them. The qualified four-branch native
family underlying the main experiment has a $21.64\%$ continuous interval. Integral recovery and replay establish
the reported chip latency separately, neither that interval nor the working
solver result establishes full-master optimality.
