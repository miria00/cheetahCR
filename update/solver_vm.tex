\section{Certificate-Producing Optimization and Design Recovery}
\label{sec:solver_vm}\label{sec:ocs}\label{sec:vm_solver}\label{sec:mccormick_blowup}
To solve the master LP we use OCS~\citep{feng2026ocs}, our in-house solver
framework. OCS is a standalone contribution whose numerical design is described
in a companion paper, with the implementation released at the cited repository
and its kernel and closing stage in Appendix~\ref{app:ocs_working_path}. Here we
give only what the results require. OCS never forms the event-expanded matrix.
It applies the constraint map $Kx$ and its adjoint $K^Ty$ as operators, runs an
anchored, restarted primal--dual scheme with a diagonal variable metric on
working supports, and then replaces the converged iterates with compact fits
whose rational reconstruction is verified against every original row. The
resulting bound is a certificate rather than a solver tolerance: the primal
satisfies the complete original branch and the dual has valid signs and
Lagrangian box support over all coordinates.

The master LP is solved by branch and bound over its declared family. Each
branch $b$ receives a certified lower bound $L_b$, branches with checked
fixed-cost infeasibility or bounds that cannot improve the best feasible solution are pruned,
and $L=\min_bL_b$ with the best feasible cost $U$ brackets the optimum. For the frozen
1,557-branch DeepSeek master LP, composing the 1,787 per-branch certificates
gives $L\approx946{,}487.72$ ms and $U\approx947{,}123.74$ ms, a relative gap of
$0.07\%$, with 1,095 branches at the stopping threshold, 462 pruned and none
unresolved (Appendix~\ref{app:expanded_native_certificate}).

\paragraph{Hardware branch and bound and recovery.}
Integral recovery searches a finite tree over array counts, vector lanes, DRAM
bandwidth, host channels and local-service units; optimistic resource and
capacity-priced retention bounds prune regions that are budget-infeasible or
cannot improve the best replayed feasible design, and leaves use actual implementation and
capacity costs. Recovery then selects one branch, integral implementations,
hardware and storage policies, and analytical replay checks dependency
readiness, capacities and service at the selected rates. The retained execution
takes $2{,}718{,}970.508466$ ms; the continuous upper above is not its latency.
Reciprocal/product relaxation and integral recovery gaps remain, so the
continuous certificate does not establish discrete chip/schedule optimality
(Appendix~\ref{app:native_solve_workflow}).
