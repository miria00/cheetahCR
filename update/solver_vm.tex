\section{Certificate-Producing Optimization and Design Recovery}
\label{sec:solver_vm}\label{sec:ocs}\label{sec:vm_solver}\label{sec:mccormick_blowup}
CHEETAH separates candidate discovery from acceptance against the complete LP.
We solve the frozen 1,557-branch DeepSeek master to a certified $0.067154\%$
relative objective gap using compact numerical fits, rational reconstruction
and original-domain checks. The closing fits use CPU HiGHS; integral design
recovery and the earlier OCS working-support experiments are separate stages.

\paragraph{Implicit representation preserves the decision domain.}
An operator evaluates a linear constraint map $Kx$ without storing its matrix;
its adjoint $K^Ty$ accumulates row prices onto coordinates. CHEETAH's generated
incidence reuses coefficients while preserving independent invocation choices.
For certification, rational temporal fields and affine interval identities
represent the primal and verify residuals over every original event. This
avoids allocating the event-expanded matrix or a dense temporal witness.

\paragraph{Compact fits discover primal and dual candidates.}
Signed coverage prices are fitted against each family's actual implementation
menus, including coincident ancestral alternatives. The final closing stage
uses 45,085 nonempty-family HiGHS solves across eight CPU workers; six empty
families require no call. Their cover is reconstructed on a $2^{32}$ rational
grid. A stateful lift preserves a causal prefix and pays for the copies,
evictions, workspace and reservations required by the changed suffix choices.
One further HiGHS fit, with 10,245 variables and 112,633 nonzeros, adjusts shared
hardware, reciprocal/service fields and original KV alternatives. Its restricted
support generates a candidate upper; its optimum is not a full-master lower.

\paragraph{Complete-domain acceptance supplies the certificate.}
The primal must satisfy the complete original branch, including initial and
terminal state, readiness, resource and physical rows. Dual multipliers must
have their original signs and valid Lagrangian box support over all coordinates;
implementation inequalities and temporal identities cover the complete source.
Each branch has a checked lower $L_b$, and the perspective composition gives
$L=\min_b L_b$. Separate saved-point reconstruction and replay, followed by
composition of 1,787 committed receipts, establish
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 L\approx946{,}487.719081842\ \mathrm{ms},\qquad
 U\approx947{,}123.742096447\ \mathrm{ms},\\
 \frac{U-L}{U}\,100\%\approx0.0671531064\%.
 \end{gathered}
\]
All 1,557 branches remain: 1,095 meet the stopping threshold and 462 have
checked fixed-cost infeasibility. No branch remains unresolved at the requested
accuracy. The certificate covers this frozen variable-hardware LP, rather than
only the compact numerical supports (Appendix~\ref{app:expanded_native_certificate}).

\paragraph{The OCS solver.}
OCS is our in-house first-order method for this program: an anchored, restarted
primal--dual hybrid gradient scheme with a diagonal variable metric that
rescales the primal and dual step sizes by the smoothed square root of the
dual-to-primal residual ratio. That rescaling is what makes primal and dual
progress practical on a system whose coefficients span ten orders of magnitude.
It applies $K$ and $K^T$ as operators with reversible scaling and JAX kernels,
so no event-expanded matrix is formed
\citep{chambolle2011first,halpern1967fixed,applegate2022practicallargescalelinearprogramming,jax2018github}.
On a 36,508-column, 313,061-row residency support it reaches a checked
$0.0774\%$ interval in 313.25 seconds on one A100, and
Appendix~\ref{app:ocs_working_path} gives the kernel. The closing certificate
above was instead discovered by the compact CPU fits and source verification of
this section; support and proof directions were selected manually, so automatic
discovery for arbitrary instances is not established.

\paragraph{Hardware branch and bound.}
Integral recovery searches a finite tree over array counts, vector lanes,
DRAM bandwidth, host channels and local-service units. Optimistic resource
and capacity-priced retention bounds prune budget-infeasible regions or those
unable to improve a replayed incumbent. Leaves use actual implementation and
capacity costs. This demonstrated tree covers a declared hardware grid with
conditional software choices; it does not branch over every discrete master
selector or solve the complete temporal LP at every node.

\paragraph{Recovery and replay.}
Recovery selects one branch, integral implementations, hardware and storage
policies. Analytical replay checks dependency readiness, capacities and service
at the selected rates. The retained later execution takes
$2{,}718{,}970.508466$ ms; the continuous upper above is not its latency.
A lift of this execution establishes its membership, not a universal relaxation
embedding for all executable designs. Reciprocal/product relaxation and integral
recovery gaps remain, so the continuous certificate does not establish discrete
chip/schedule optimality. Appendix~\ref{app:native_solve_workflow} records the
solve and recovery procedures separately.
