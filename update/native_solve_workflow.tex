\subsection{End-to-End Solution and Recovery Workflow}
\label{app:native_solve_workflow}
The completed continuous solve uses a CHEETAH-specific certificate-producing
procedure. Compact working problems discover witnesses; the complete original
LP determines acceptance. The method retains an explicit record of manually
chosen supports and proof directions. It is not a demonstrated automatic
branch-and-price or full-master PDHG algorithm.

\paragraph{1. Freeze the mathematical instance.}
The expanded family contains 1,557 whole-program branches, retaining all
61,985,244 independent native invocations and 62,030,334 logical nodes including
conversions. The admitted branch has 15,044,386,833 implementation choices and
5,809,955,157 original event labels. Hardware ranges, physical rows, source
coefficients, original boxes and the declared bank/product relaxation remain
fixed throughout closing. Generated coefficient classes share arithmetic,
not invocation decisions. Implementations outside this identified library
are outside the certificate.

\paragraph{2. Fit coverage prices against actual source menus.}
The compiler enumerates every real ancestral implementation on its original
membership, saved tile, representation and eligibility conditions. Coincident
memberships retain all genuine implementation options. Original exact-cover
equalities have free multipliers; inequality multipliers keep their required
signs. The final closing stage uses 45,085 finite CPU HiGHS fits over the
nonempty families, with eight workers; six empty families require no solve.
Complete checks cover 31,706,528 weighted invocations and 39,223,145 group
inequalities. Analytic KV, conversion and prefill prices complete the source
coverage construction under one shared resource budget.

\paragraph{3. Reconstruct and lift a stateful primal.}
Numerical cover marginals are reconstructed on a $2^{32}$ rational grid;
integer incidence arithmetic verifies exact coverage of every original member.
Literal source compilation binds 12,264,122 positive original coordinates and
255 actual coefficient templates. A causal prefix supplies mass
$f=1/41{,}969{,}600$ of the inherited execution. The suffix supplies the
remaining mass through the new cover, preserving independent coordinates and
original event labels. The selector is $f y^{\rm prefix}+(1-f)y^{\rm suffix}$;
coverage alone is insufficient to establish feasibility.

The lift therefore reconstructs owner states, readiness and all required
transfers. It mirrors 25,607,232 nonterminal owners between their existing
SRAM/DRAM tiers, pays initialization and suffix copies, and preserves original
initial inventory, KV history and terminal backing. One further CPU HiGHS
program jointly adjusts original KV alternatives, shared hardware, reciprocal
variables and service epigraphs on this candidate construction. Its 10,245
variables, 1,039 equalities, 104 inequalities and 112,633 nonzeros cover 1,022
KV classes. Reusing a coefficient-class mixture restricts this candidate search,
not the frozen master; its optimizer objective supplies no unrestricted lower.

\paragraph{4. Check the original domain and compose the certificate.}
For $\min c^Tx$ subject to $Ex=e$, $Gx\le g$, $x\in[\ell,u]$, the
Lagrangian convention used by the checker gives, for $\mu\ge0$ and free $\nu$,
\begin{equation}
 L(\nu,\mu)=-e^T\nu-g^T\mu+
   \inf_{\ell\le x\le u}(c+E^T\nu+G^T\mu)^Tx.
 \label{eq:complete_box_bound}
\end{equation}
All original coordinate bounds, residual signs and recession directions enter
this calculation. In particular, a tiny negative residual on an unbounded
nonnegative coordinate cannot be rounded to zero. Restricted multiplier search
is permitted because each returned lower is evaluated over the complete
original domain. An omitted-column working dual alone is insufficient.

The primal checker verifies the complete source lift, using exact rational
fields and affine temporal identities rather than materializing all events.
Saved-point reconstruction and separate replay reproduce the objective and
proof. The final adopted receipt also includes the explicit staging-reservation
supplement. Complete branch proofs, including zero-weight perspective cases,
give $L=\min_b L_b$; the best admitted branch supplies $U$. We stop at the
first checked $(U-L)/U\le10^{-3}$. The final composition checks committed proof
receipts and identities; it does not rerun every inherited checker. Shared
checker algorithms and manual support selection are recorded limitations of
automation and independent implementation, not missing terms in the certificate.
Section~\ref{sec:ocs} gives the accepted endpoints.

\paragraph{5. Search discrete hardware and recover software.}
The hardware branch-and-bound tree is separate from the whole-program branch
index $b$ in the LP. A tree node is a rectangle in a declared finite menu of
array counts, vector lanes, DRAM rates, host channels and local-service units.
Minimum-resource budget violations prune infeasible rectangles. Optimistic
resource values, relaxed private-capacity constraints and repriced local
implementations provide latency lower estimates. In a joint variant, each
parameter family has a two-state retained/streamed dynamic program. Nonnegative
prices on shared capacity rows yield the conditional Lagrangian bound
\[
 L(h,\mu)=\sum_f\min_{\pi\in\mathcal P_f}
       \left[t_f(h,\pi)+\sum_t\mu_t B_{ft}(\pi)\right]
       -\sum_t\mu_t C_t
\]
plus costs outside those paths. Here $f$ identifies an original parameter
family, $\pi$ an allowed retention path, and $B_{ft}$ its storage use.
GPU scans evaluate the independent paths; shared prices preserve the capacity
coupling in the bound. A path mixture or optimistic rectangle is not itself
an executable schedule.

The demonstrated menu contains 536,380,416 hardware combinations:
64--1,024 arrays in steps of two, 1,024--16,384 vector lanes in steps of 128,
512--2,560 GB/s DRAM in steps of 128 plus 2,608 GB/s, 1--64 host channels,
and 1--8 local-service units. An earlier fixed-software tree exhausted that
menu; the joint capacity-path tree made 1,190 GPU oracle calls and retained
142 unresolved schedule leaves, reduced to 78 by its subsequent replayed
best feasible design. Its bounds were numerical and conditional on the then-current
cover, progress and implementation menu. They are not the final LP certificate,
and subsequent menu expansions require new tree bounds.

Only a replayed feasible design supplies a pruning reference; an unverified
candidate does not. Surviving hardware choices undergo integral cover/path
selection, capacity repair and transfer scheduling. Replay then computes
the predicted phase latencies using the selected chip's actual modeled
rates, clocks, formats and buffer reservations. This is how an integral
hardware/software result is obtained without claiming a completed tree over
every master selector.

\paragraph{What an exhaustive discrete proof would additionally require.}
One would need complete lower bounds at every remaining hardware/schedule node,
pricing over every allowed implementation under each branching decision,
and a fully explored or pruned frontier. Making tile or branch choices integral
alone does not make continuous rate/product envelopes exact. Spatial branching
to enforce physical products addresses a stronger nonlinear design problem;
it is not necessary to solve the frozen LP relaxation and is not claimed here.
Analytical replay supports the reported design latency, while fabrication and
global discrete optimality remain separate questions.
