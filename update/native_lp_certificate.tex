\paragraph{Explicit dual certificate.}
Use the notation of Eq.~\eqref{eq:master}. Let $\kappa$ be the stored
duration-cost coefficient, $u_b^-$ the stored inverse-bandwidth lower-support
coefficient in branch $b$, $B_k$ the original stream demand of implementation
$k$, and $\pi_i\ge0$ a logical node's coverage price, including conversion nodes.
We verify $\sum_{i\in S_k}\pi_i\le B_k$ for every implementation in every
frozen generator. At branch weight one, under the convention
$c^Tx+\sum_r \mu_r(A_rx-b_r)$, the nonzero row multipliers are
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 Z^B_e-d_e\le0: & \mu=\kappa,\\
 u_b^- Q^B_e-Z^B_e\le0: & \mu=\kappa,\\
 Q^B_e-\sum_k B_k w_{ke}-\text{transfer bytes}_e=0:
   & \mu=-\kappa u_b^-,\\
 \sum_e w_{ke}-y_k=0: & \mu=-\kappa u_b^- B_k,\\
 \sum_{k:i\in S_k}y_k=1: & \mu=-\kappa u_b^- \pi_i.
\end{array}
\]
All other multipliers are zero. The duration, service, demand and work
coefficients cancel. Choice residuals are
$\kappa u_b^-(B_k-\sum_{i\in S_k}\pi_i)\ge0$; transfer residuals are nonnegative.
Original nonnegative coordinate bounds therefore give
$L_b=\kappa u_b^-\sum_i\pi_i$. For the perspective branch union, the multiplier
$-\min_b L_b$ on $\sum_b\lambda_b=1$ leaves residual
$L_b-\min_b L_b\ge0$, proving $L=\min_bL_b$ for the full family.
The multiplier identities apply to every original event, including events
outside the primal's compact parent support.
Here $\kappa=4722366482869645/4722366482869645213696$ is the exact
stored binary64 value of $10^{-6}$; $u_b^-$ likewise uses the stored outward
coefficient. Ideal decimal constants are not substituted into the certificate.
