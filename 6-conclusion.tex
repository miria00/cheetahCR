\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
CHEETAH jointly represents hardware, implementation, and temporal memory
choices in one master LP relaxation. Compact numerical fits, complete-domain
certificates and integral replay connect the relaxation to concrete designs. Full-input captures and
matched analytical controls expose prefill, decode and memory-reuse effects.
The frozen 1,557-branch DeepSeek master is solved to a certified $0.067154\%$
objective gap, while the recovered chip remains a separately replayed analytical
design. Broader automation, calibrated cost assumptions and global discrete
optimality remain open. The accepted original campaign retains its own
performance comparisons and validation evidence.
