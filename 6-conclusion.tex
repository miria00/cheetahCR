\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
CHEETAH presents a new paradigm for hardware--software co-design tooling: a single
master LP relaxation that unifies hardware sizing, implementation and tiling,
fusion, time-indexed placement, weight and KV residency, rematerialization and
scheduling, and more. These are decision spaces that prior methods cover only sequentially in part
(Table~\ref{tab:capability-matrix}). On the analysis side, our containment theorem shows that
CHEETAH subsumes rather than approximates the fusion search it generalizes.
Branch and bound over the declared family and complete-domain certificates
connect the relaxation to concrete designs, thus the
1,557-branch DeepSeek master LP is solved to a certified $0.07\%$ gap.
Reported chips must pass by six pre-RTL feasibility gates whose
constants carry an audited provenance trail. CHEETAH's new mathematical abstraction for co-design 
presents a novel
framework, though much future work remains (Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}). The reported
speedups are reflective of analytical cost models to be consistent with prior work, supported by RTL and
synthesis evidence. Cross-GEMM fusion,
unrestricted overlap, and calibration of the cost model against fabricated silicon remain open.
