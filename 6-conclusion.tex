\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
CHEETAH jointly represents hardware, implementation, and temporal memory
choices in one master LP relaxation. Operator-based optimization, checked
bounds, and integral replay produce concrete designs. Full-input DeepSeek demonstrates $22.88\times$ overall acceleration
under the modeled TPU-v3, with separate phase and feature controls.
