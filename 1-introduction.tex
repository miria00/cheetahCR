\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
AI inference demand is expected to grow by $10{,}000\times$ over the next five
years~\citep{cra2026aifuture}. General-purpose accelerators leave significant
efficiency gains unrealized, which motivates accelerators specialized to their
target workloads. Specialization, however, requires coordinating datapaths,
memory capacity, mappings, and compiler transformations, and the resulting
design space can exceed $\mathcal O(10^{2300})$~\citep{zhang_fast}. Engineers typically tackle this problem in
stages: propose hardware, map each operation, then optimize data reuse and fusion. However this sequential method misses crucial global gains in co-design. 
For example, a locally faster tile can consume the memory needed to retain a neighboring
tensor, and a slightly slower tile can therefore yield a faster complete schedule. 


In this paper, we introduce \textsc{CHEETAH}: \acroletter{C}o-designed \acroletter{H}ardware
\acroletter{E}xploration via \acroletter{E}fficient \acroletter{T}hroughput and
\acroletter{H}ybrid-search, a novel mathematically principled hardware-software co-design framework with LLM guided search efficiency.
CHEETAH is a new paradigm for co-design. Rather than adding one more stage to
the sequential pipeline, it unifies in a single formulation the decision spaces
that prior hardware and software methods each cover only in part;
Table~\ref{tab:capability-matrix} positions it against nine such systems. The
unification is not only descriptive: Theorem~\ref{thm:containment} in
Appendix~\ref{app:fast_containment} proves that the lifted feasible set of
FAST's fusion search embeds in CHEETAH's relaxation at the same objective, so
the formulation subsumes that search rather than merely resembling it.
The inner loop consists of one master convex program which exposes complex interactions
jointly. Its implementation choices determine work, traffic, memory,
time-indexed states track residency and reuse. Its shared hardware variables
allocate compute, vector, memory, and bandwidth resources under area and power
budgets. CHEETAH uses McCormick envelopes and disaggregated epigraph techniques to model HW/SW relationships in a rigorous and realistic way.
The large scale convex program also yields bounds for its declared domain, while integral recovery
and replay verify each chip designed and complementing software schedule. These are distinct
outputs, since a single low fractional objective alone is not a realistic executable physical design.

The CHEETAH formulation operates on a graph of independent logical invocations and
typed storage objects. Convolutions, dense and expert GEMMs, attention, and
vector operations contribute implementation-specific coefficients and
dependencies. Optional batches and fusion groups cover original invocations, and KV prefixes, expert weights, and long skip
connections enter the same capacity and readiness accounting. This makes the
program reusable across architectures without assuming that identical shapes
must share a decision.

We separate inference acceleration gains betwee prefill and decode. Prefill amortizes weight traffic across many tokens, whil autoregressive decode
repeatedly accesses weights and an expanding KV history. Batching helps, but
sparse routing also increases the number of experts touched. Under independent
uniform top-$k$ routing, the expected union is
$n[1-(1-k/n)^B]$: with $n=256$, $k=8$, it grows from $8$ experts at batch one
to about $163$ at batch $32$. Thus batching does not guarantee a compute-bound
decode phase. CHEETAH therefore jointly chooses resource provisioning, compatible
batches, and temporal residency. Our full-inference experiment uses actual
routes, retains the complete KV history, and charges every restore. Its gains
do not assume that all weights or KV fit on chip.

The complete CHEETAH program is too large to materialize. To solve the
large-scale linear program we develop a specialized in-house method, named OCS~\citep{feng2026ocs}.
The master is solved by \emph{branch and bound} over its declared branch family.
Each branch is a whole-program node carrying its own hardware and execution
semantics, so the family is the search tree and a node is a complete LP in its
own right. At a node, compact CPU LP fits discover coverage prices and primal
candidates, rational reconstruction supplies a stateful point, and symbolic
checks verify the complete original domain, which certifies a valid lower bound
$L_b$ for that node. A node is pruned when it carries checked fixed-cost
infeasibility or when its bound cannot improve the replayed incumbent, so no
descendant need be expanded. The surviving minimum $L=\min_bL_b$ and the
incumbent cost $U$ bracket the optimum, and the search stops on their relative
gap. The resulting 1,557-branch DeepSeek LP is certified to a $0.067154\%$
objective gap.
An LLM architect outer loop then reads resource
prices and trial diagnostics to propose target specific implementations or hardware search neighborhoods. This reduces the reliance on brute force large space search effectively. Crucially, the hardware-software interactions and decisions are captured in the CHEETAH inner loop formulation. 

\paragraph{Contributions.} CHEETAH combines a unified HW/SW design relaxation,
operator-based optimization and certified bounds with an LLM architect that
proposes implementations and hardware search neighborhoods.
\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{CHEETAH formulation.}
    We introduce time-indexed LP variables $p_{i,t}^k$ and rematerialization variables $r_{i,t}$ to enable cheap recompute operations instead of paying DRAM reload costs, supporting skip connections and multi-fanout nodes.
    We unify tiling and fusion through \textbf{McCormick envelopes} and resource
    epigraphs. Binary tile--residency products are exact at integral choices;
    continuous hardware/service products retain their explicit relaxation.
    These couplings expose tiling--placement trade-offs that sequential
    pipelines can discard.
    % This enables the LP to discover trade-offs where accepting a suboptimal tiling configuration enables superior fusion, or vice versa---precisely the cross-stage interactions that the sequential pipeline cannot capture.
    \item \textbf{Structure-aware optimization, bounds and executable
    validation.} Compact numerical
    fits, rational cover reconstruction and stateful lifting make candidate
    discovery manageable. Original-domain checks certify the complete LP
    without substituting restricted-support optima for full-master bounds:
    the frozen 1,557-branch DeepSeek master has a certified $0.067154\%$ LP
    objective gap. Analytical replay and selected control/datapath checks
    support a separate recovered design; global discrete optimality remains
    open.
    Section~\ref{sec:solver_vm} summarizes OCS, the in-house solver framework
    whose closing stage certifies these bounds; its details are in the appendix
    and a separate paper.

    \item \textbf{LLM Architect Outer Loop.} The architect interprets resource prices and a causal ledger of earlier trials to propose hardware and implementation changes. Deterministic checks admit proposals; the LLM itself supplies no certificate. This couples architecture exploration with the optimizer's diagnostics rather than relying only on black-box scores.

    \item \textbf{Inference speedup.} Against unchanged naive TPU-v3,
    CHEETAH predicts up to $11.85\times$ for EfficientNet forward execution
    and the SD1.5 UNet denoiser, up to $24.20\times$ for LLM prefill, and up
    to $17.93\times$ for LLM decode (Figure~\ref{fig:speedup}).
    The decode-only
    designs predict $7.82\times$ for DeepSeek-V3 and
    $11.09$--$17.93\times$ for the three Llamas; the
    prefill-oriented designs report an average $22.07\times$ and up to
    $40.1\times$ under their own cost conventions
    (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}). These are analytical
    matrix-and-associated-traffic results with the TPU-v3 timing proxy,
    not measured TPU-v3 serving gains. Their area proxies are below
    600~mm$^2$, but relaxed memory/power assumptions remain physically
    unqualified. The prefill and decode-only designs are different chips.
\end{enumerate}

The following paper is organized as follows. Section~\ref{sec:related} discusses related work, Section~\ref{sec:prelim} summarizes preliminary background,  Section~\ref{sec:methods}
presents the CHEETAH master and its features, and Section~\ref{sec:ocs} describes
the OCS solver framework, its certificate and hardware search. Section~\ref{sec:experiments}
reports experimental results and ablations, and the appendix provides containment
theorems, physical-validation details, co-design blueprints, and reproduction evidence.
