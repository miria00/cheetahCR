\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
AI inference demand is expected to grow by $10{,}000\times$ over the next five
years~\citep{cra2026aifuture}, and general-purpose accelerators leave much of
the attainable efficiency unrealized, which motivates accelerators specialized to
their workloads. A specialized accelerator is not one decision but many coupled
ones: datapath and memory sizing, per-operation tiling, which tensors stay
resident and when, what is fused, and what is recomputed rather than reloaded.
These choices interact through shared capacity and bandwidth, and jointly they
span a design space exceeding $\mathcal O(10^{2300})$
configurations~\citep{zhang_fast}, far too large to enumerate, simulate, or
materialize as one explicit program. Existing flows therefore decompose the
problem: they fix a hardware configuration, map each operation in isolation, and
then optimize reuse and fusion on what remains. Every prior co-design flow shares this
sequential pipeline, and it is why the joint problem has stayed open: each stage
commits before the global memory consequences of its choice are known, and the
commitment is irreversible downstream. The failure is concrete. A mapper that
exports its fastest isolated tile can consume exactly the on-chip memory needed
to keep a neighboring tensor resident, so the locally optimal tile forces a DRAM
round trip and the complete schedule is slower. A slightly slower tile would
have produced the faster program. No stage of the pipeline can see this
trade-off, because it exists only in the joint space.

In this paper we introduce \textbf{\textsc{CHEETAH}}: \acroletter{C}o-designed
\acroletter{H}ardware \acroletter{E}xploration via \acroletter{E}fficient
\acroletter{T}hroughput and \acroletter{H}ybrid-search, a mathematically
principled hardware--software co-design framework with LLM-guided search.
CHEETAH is a new paradigm for co-design. Rather than adding one more stage to
the sequential pipeline (Figure~\ref{fig::workflow}), it unifies in a single
CHEETAH master linear program (LP) the decision spaces that prior hardware and
software methods each cover only in part. Table~\ref{tab:capability-matrix}
positions it against nine such systems, and Theorem~\ref{thm:containment} in Appendix~\ref{app:fast_containment} proves
that the lifted feasible set of FAST's fusion search embeds in CHEETAH's
relaxation at the same objective, so the formulation subsumes that search rather
than resembling it. Implementation choices determine work, traffic and memory.
Time-indexed states track residency and reuse. Shared hardware variables
allocate compute, vector, memory and bandwidth under area and power budgets, and
McCormick envelopes and disaggregated epigraphs couple them. The program acts on
a graph of logical invocations and typed storage objects, so convolutions,
dense and expert GEMMs, attention, KV prefixes, expert weights and long skip
connections enter one capacity and readiness accounting, and prefill and decode
are the same program over different event sequences. Decode is where this
matters most: under independent top-$k$ routing with $n=256$, $k=8$, the
expected number of experts touched grows from $8$ at batch one to about $163$
at batch $32$, so batching alone does not make decode compute-bound and
provisioning, batching and temporal residency must be chosen together.

Three properties of this problem defeat off-the-shelf solving. It does not fit
in memory: Llama-1 13B's root alone is $10.1$M columns by $18.3$M rows, its
full execution adds $520$M rows, and stored densely it would occupy 51\,PB,
while the design space exceeds $10^{2300}$ branches, so the program must be
generated on demand rather than built. Its structure is extreme: almost every
row is a one-hot cover, an epigraph or a McCormick envelope, with 3.4 nonzeros
per row at every scale and density below $0.01\%$, and rows outnumber columns
three to one, so the optimal face is massively degenerate and cost tracks
degeneracy rather than size (three times the rows costs six times the time).
Its conditioning is hostile: coefficients span $10^{-6}$ to $10^{15}$, a single
reciprocal rate variable spans $80\times$ inside its own box, and standard
first-order methods plateau at a $0.999$ primal violation after 2,000
iterations and never descend, even with the chip pinned. A certificate
therefore cannot be read off floating-point iterates. It has to be built in
exact rational arithmetic. What makes the joint space tractable is that it
never has to be materialized. CHEETAH solves the master LP by branch and bound
over a declared family of whole-program branches using OCS~\citep{feng2026ocs},
our in-house operator solver. Each branch is a complete LP whose constraint map
is applied rather than stored. Compact fits discover prices and primal
candidates, and symbolic checks over the original domain certify a lower bound
$L_b$ for the branch. Branches that are infeasible or cannot beat the replayed
incumbent are pruned, and $L=\min_bL_b$ with the incumbent $U$ brackets the
optimum. The 1,557-branch DeepSeek-V3 master LP closes to a certified $0.07\%$
gap. Integral recovery and replay then turn the relaxation into a chip and a
schedule, which are outputs distinct from the bound. An LLM architect reads the
resulting resource prices and trial diagnostics to propose implementations and
hardware neighborhoods in place of brute-force outer search. The
hardware--software interactions themselves are captured in the inner
formulation, not in the LLM.

\paragraph{Contributions.} CHEETAH combines a unified HW/SW design relaxation,
operator-based optimization and certified bounds with an LLM architect that
proposes implementations and hardware search neighborhoods.
\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{CHEETAH formulation.}
    We introduce time-indexed LP variables $p_{i,t}^k$ and rematerialization variables $r_{i,t}$ to enable cheap recompute operations instead of paying DRAM reload costs, supporting skip connections and multi-fanout nodes.
    We unify tiling and fusion through \textbf{McCormick envelopes} and resource
    epigraphs. Binary tile--residency products are exact at integral choices;
    continuous hardware/service products retain their explicit relaxation.
    These couplings expose tiling--placement trade-offs that sequential
    pipelines can discard.
    % This enables the LP to discover trade-offs where accepting a suboptimal tiling configuration enables superior fusion, or vice versa---precisely the cross-stage interactions that the sequential pipeline cannot capture.
    \item \textbf{Certified optimization and executable validation.} Compact
    numerical fits, rational cover reconstruction and original-domain checks
    certify the complete master LP rather than a restricted support, and
    analytical replay validates a separately recovered design
    (Section~\ref{sec:solver_vm}). Global discrete optimality remains open.

    \item \textbf{LLM Architect Outer Loop.} The architect interprets resource prices and a causal ledger of earlier trials to propose hardware and implementation changes. Deterministic checks admit proposals; the LLM itself supplies no certificate. This couples architecture exploration with the optimizer's diagnostics rather than relying only on black-box scores.

    \item \textbf{Inference speedup.} Across 12 workloads, CHEETAH co-designs
    chips that outperform the naive TPU-v3 baseline by an order of magnitude on
    vision, LLM prefill and LLM decode (Figure~\ref{fig:speedup},
    Section~\ref{sec:experiments}). These are analytical results under the
    TPU-v3 timing proxy, not measured serving gains, and the prefill and decode
    designs are different chips.
\end{enumerate}

The following paper is organized as follows. Section~\ref{sec:related} discusses related work, Section~\ref{sec:prelim} summarizes preliminary background,  Section~\ref{sec:methods}
presents the master LP and its features, and Section~\ref{sec:ocs} describes
the OCS solver framework, its certificate and hardware search. Section~\ref{sec:experiments}
reports experimental results and ablations, and the appendix provides containment
theorems, physical-validation details, co-design blueprints, and reproduction evidence.
