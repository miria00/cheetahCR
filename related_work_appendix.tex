\section{Related Work}
\label{app:related_work}\label{related_work_appendix}\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Accelerator design tools.}
Accelerator mappers such as Timeloop, CoSA, and ZigZag explore tiling and
mapping, and FAST connects hardware search, mapping, and fusion in a staged
flow~\citep{timeloop,huang2021cosaschedulingconstrainedoptimization,mei2021zigzag,zhang_fast}.
DOSA jointly differentiates hardware and mapping choices, while Gemini explores
chiplet architectures and layer pipelines~\citep{hong2023dosa,cai2024gemini}.
TileFlow and FADiff address fusion-aware dataflows and joint mapping--fusion
optimization~\citep{zheng2023tileflow,jia2025fadifffusionawaredifferentiableoptimization}.
CHEETAH couples these choices to temporal storage and shared hardware in an LP
relaxation. LLM proposal systems such as FunSearch and
AlphaEvolve~\citep{romera2024mathematical,novikov2025alphaevolvecodingagentscientific}
inform the outer-loop role: propose changes, then evaluate them independently. This is its distinction in formulation.
Datacenter ASICs and the TPU established the value of
specialization~\citep{jouppi2017datacenter,magaki2016asic,xie2018extreme}, and
LLMCompass, Chiplet Cloud and Theseus size serving hardware for large language
models at the system and wafer
scale~\citep{zhang2024llmcompass,peng2024chipletcloudbuildingai,zhu2024theseusexploringefficientwaferscale}.

\paragraph{Architecture and mapping search.}
Timeloop searches mappings for a supplied architecture~\citep{timeloop}.
Interstellar, dMazeRunner, MAESTRO and MAGNet likewise analyze or generate
dataflows for a fixed accelerator template, and Eyeriss~v2 fixes a flexible
dataflow in silicon~\citep{yang_interstellar,dave2019dmazerunner,kwon2020understandingreuseperformancehardware,venkatesan2019magnet,chen2019eyerissv2flexibleaccelerator}.
CoSA uses mixed-integer optimization for a constrained mapping objective, but
its guarantee does not make the resulting latency globally optimal under an
arbitrary simulator~\citep{huang2021cosaschedulingconstrainedoptimization}.
ZigZag expands the architecture--mapping space~\citep{mei2021zigzag}, while
DOSA places architecture and mapping variables in one differentiable program
\citep{hong2023dosa}. Gemini couples spatial mappings and architectural choices
for multi-chiplet accelerators~\citep{cai2024gemini}. CODEBench uses
multi-objective exploration over accelerator configurations~\citep{Tuli_2023}.
HASCO, ConfuciuX, Apollo, Unico and learned co-design apply agile, reinforcement
or learned search to the hardware and software
spaces~\citep{xiao2021hasco,kao2020confuciuxautonomoushardwareresource,yazdanbakhsh2021apollotransferablearchitectureexploration,rashidi2023unico,shi2020learnedhardwaresoftwarecodesignneural}.
FAST combines hardware, mapping, and compiler search, including a fusion ILP
\citep{zhang_fast}. These methods motivate our restricted controls.
CHEETAH retains alternatives until shared storage, implementation cost, and
hardware provisioning can be evaluated together.

\paragraph{Fusion, placement, and rematerialization.}
DeFiNES includes depth-first fused execution, caching, and overlap recomputation.
Its restricted placement and recomputation cells in Table~\ref{tab:capability-matrix}
are therefore not marked absent~\citep{mei2023defines}. Fast-and-Fusiest searches
legal operation orders as well as fusion and mappings under its stated cost model
\citep{andrulis2026fusiest}. These are substantial joint compiler capabilities,
even though they do not provide CHEETAH's complete temporal state model and
variable hardware domain. Checkmate formulates training checkpointing as an
integer program~\citep{jain2020checkmatebreakingmemorywall}, and POET combines
rematerialization and paging~\citep{patil2022poettrainingneuralnetworks}.
Sublinear-memory checkpointing, Moccasin, Coop and BladeDISC++ trade
recomputation or symbolic-shape planning for
footprint~\citep{chen2016trainingdeepnetssublinear,bartan2023moccasinefficienttensorrematerialization,zhang2023coopmemorycommodity,yuan2024bladediscmemoryoptimizationsbased}.
Operator-fusion compilers such as FusionStitching, the oneDNN Graph Compiler,
Neptune, RedFuser and Multi-DOF fusion, and polyhedral fusion heuristics,
exploit locality on fixed
hardware~\citep{zheng2021fusionstitchingboostingmemoryintensive,li2024onednngraphcompilerhybrid,zhao2026neptuneadvancedmloperator,Tang_2026,chen_multi_dof,acharya2020fusion}.
KV-cache eviction such as StreamingLLM and H2O, and diffusion feature caching
such as DeepCache, shrink inference state at a quality cost, whereas CHEETAH
keeps every entry and prices its
placement~\citep{xiao2024streamingllm,zhang2023h2o,ma2024deepcache}.
CHEETAH uses paid recomputation, transfer completion, and readiness to represent
these tradeoffs for inference. Representability alone is not a measured gain.
Our native controls exercise only the features identified in
Appendix~\ref{app:rows_scope}.

\paragraph{Polyhedral transformations.}
Affine scheduling~\citep{feautrier1992affine} underlies this family. Pluto
constructs affine schedules for parallelism and locality
\citep{bondhugula2008pluto}, and Halide and Ansor search schedules for fixed
hardware~\citep{ragan2013halide,zheng2023ansorgeneratinghighperformancetensor}. Tiramisu exposes a richer programmable scheduling
language, including storage and recomputation transformations
\citep{baghdadi2019tiramisu}. Their user-programmed capabilities should not be
confused with an automatic joint search over every table axis. Conversely,
CHEETAH's causal event choices should not be described as arbitrary loop
transformation coverage: its implementation library and dependency contract
bound the represented schedules.

\paragraph{Relaxations and first-order LP solvers.}
McCormick envelopes bound bilinear products over boxes
\citep{mccormick1976computability}. Exactness for a single integral-factor
product does not prove exactness of an entire fractional co-design program.
PDLP demonstrates practical large-scale linear programming with first-order
methods~\citep{applegate2022practicallargescalelinearprogramming,applegate2025pdlp}, and
CvxCluster solves large granular resource-allocation programs at similar
scale~\citep{cvxcluster2026}. GPU variants
include cuPDLP.jl, cuPDLP-C, and cuPDLPx
\citep{lu2024cupdlpjlgpuimplementationrestarted,lu2024cupdlpcstrengthenedimplementationcupdlp,lu2025cupdlpxenhancedgpubasedfirstorder}.
OCS, our in-house solver framework~\citep{feng2026ocs}, is described in a
companion paper. Its certified objective interval establishes the frozen LP
accuracy independently of any single iterate's tolerance. It does not establish
a general automatic solver or discrete-design optimality.

\paragraph{LLM-guided search.}
FunSearch and AlphaEvolve combine model proposals with deterministic evaluation
\citep{romera2024mathematical,novikov2025alphaevolvecodingagentscientific}.
EvoPrompting, LUMINA, LIMCA and Hawkeye apply language models to architecture,
GPU and kernel exploration, and evolutionary algorithm-discovery frameworks
generalize the proposal-then-evaluate
loop~\citep{chen2023evopromptinglanguagemodelscodelevel,zhang2026luminallmguidedgpuarchitecture,vungarala2025limcallmautomatinganalog,tschand2026hawkeye,surina2025algorithmdiscoveryllmsevolutionary,skydiscover2026}.
CHEETAH similarly uses an LLM to propose search interventions, with resource
prices and failed-trial diagnostics as context. The outer loop edits
Vizier search neighborhoods, and current master experiments can also propose
implementations and hardware alternatives directly. A proposal is admitted
only after its numerical, dependency, and resource checks. The proposal
mechanism is not an exhaustive separation oracle.
