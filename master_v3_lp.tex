\section{Complete Rows of the Master Blocks}
\label{app:master_rows}\label{app:master_v3}
Equation~\eqref{eq:master} is a perspective formulation of a declared finite
family. If branch $b$ has equality matrix $E_b$, inequality matrix $G_b$,
right-hand sides $e_b,g_b$, and coordinate box $[\ell_b,u_b]$, its complete
block form is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \min_{\lambda,x}\quad&
 \sum_{b\in\mathcal B_C}\kappa_b\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_b}d_{be}
 +\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_P}\alpha_b
       \left(\sum_iT_{bi}+\sum_{i,t}c^{\rm remat}_{bi}r_{bit}\right)\\
 \text{s.t.}\quad&E_bx_b=e_b\lambda_b,\quad G_bx_b\le g_b\lambda_b,\\
 &\ell_{b\nu}\lambda_b\le x_{b\nu}\le u_{b\nu}\lambda_b,\quad
   \lambda_b\ge0,\quad\sum_b\lambda_b=1.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:master_block}
\end{equation}
This is the explicit coefficient expansion of $\sum_b c_b^Tx_b$: duration
coordinates carry cost $\kappa_b$ in causal branches; operation latency and
rematerialization carry their original costs in retained schedule branches.
All other coordinates in these blocks have zero objective cost. Branch weights
are not multiplied into this expression a second time. A native-only instance
has $\mathcal B_P=\varnothing$. The declared row matrices include the direct
physical constraints below precisely when that versioned branch includes them.
Only finite endpoints generate box rows. A branch at weight one retains its
original constraints and objective; setting every other branch to zero gives
an explicit inclusion map. This does not transfer a lower bound from one
branch to a larger family. The retained schedule block below explains inclusion
of the supplied joint LP when it is configured as a branch. The certified
21-branch native instance uses the causal blocks and their identified extensions;
its bound is not asserted for a union with additional schedule models or a
larger implementation library.

\subsection{The Retained Joint Tiling--Placement Block}
For original operation $i$, configuration $c$, edge $e$, and fixed time label
$t$, let $P_{ik}=p_{\mathrm{assoc}(i,k),o(i)}$ for an associated activation,
$P_{iW}=p^W_{i,o(i)}$ for weights, and $P_{ik}=0$ when no placement association
exists. The block's contribution to the master objective is
$\alpha_b(\sum_i T_i+\sum_{i,t}c_i^{\rm remat}r_{it})$; $\alpha_b$ converts its
cost units. Its explicit rows, in the original joint-LP notation, are
\begin{align*}
&\sum_c y_{ic}=\lambda_b,\qquad h_b=h_b^P\lambda_b,\\
&T_i\ge\sum_cT_i^{\min}(c)y_{ic},\qquad
 T_i\ge\sum_cT_i^{\max}(c)y_{ic}-\sum_{c,k}t_i^k(c)w_{ick},\\
&0\le w_{ick}\le y_{ic},\quad w_{ick}\le P_{ik},\quad
 w_{ick}\ge y_{ic}+P_{ik}-\lambda_b,\\
&\sum_e d_ep_{et}+\sum_i d_i^Wp^W_{it}\le C_{GM,b}\lambda_b,\\
&p_{et}\le p_{e,t-1}+r_{\mathrm{src}(e),t}\quad(t>o(\mathrm{src}(e))),\\
&p^W_{it}\le p^W_{i,t-1}\quad(t>o(i)),\qquad r_{it}=0\quad(t\le o(i)),\\
&r_{it}\le p_{et},\quad r_{it}\le p^W_{it}\quad(e\in\mathrm{in}(i),\ t>o(i)),\\
&T_i\ge0,\qquad 0\le y,w,p,p^W,r\le\lambda_b.
\end{align*}
These rows preserve the original fractional savings convention. Extra cold-start,
copy, or exact-product constraints are not appended to this block. Causal
execution uses its own complete contract instead.

\subsection{Native Cover, Lifecycle and Storage}
All following equations use scaled coordinates in one native branch.
The event range is $0\le e<E$, with storage/completion states at $0\le e\le E$.
A column covers its original nodes $S_k$, including any conversion nodes; all
cover, work-mass, active-state, typed $w=f$, control and operand-readiness rows
are those in Eq.~\eqref{eq:master}. In particular
$\sum_k a_{ke}=A_e\le\lambda_b$ is an aggregate active-mass restriction.
Distinct invocations retain distinct coverage constraints even when coefficients
are reused.

Let $O_k$ be implementation outputs and $\tau_k$ their declared destination.
For initial indicators $I_j,I_{j\tau}$, the birth and tier recurrences are
\begin{align}
 &\beta_{j0}=I_j\lambda_b,\qquad p_{j\tau0}=I_{j\tau}\lambda_b,\nonumber\\
 &\beta_{j,e+1}=\beta_{je}+\sum_{k:j\in O_k}f_{ke},\nonumber\\
 &p_{j\tau,e+1}=p_{j\tau e}-v_{j\tau e}
   +\sum_{\sigma:\sigma\to\tau}q_{j,\sigma\to\tau,e}
   +\sum_{k:j\in O_k,\tau_k=\tau}f_{ke}
   +{\bf1}_{\{\tau=D,\ j\in\mathcal J_{\rm stage}\}}\delta_{je}.
 \label{eq:native_state}
\end{align}
$H,D,S$ denote host, DRAM and SRAM. The drain term occurs only in staging
branches. Ordinary copy links are $H\leftrightarrow D\leftrightarrow S$.
A copy retains its source and adds destination state at the next event; it
is not a destructive move or instantaneous readiness. State, eviction,
copy, choice and lifecycle masses have boxes $[0,\lambda_b]$.

For a nonterminal object's original users $\mathcal U_j$, backing requires
\begin{align}
 &\beta_{je}\le p_{jHe}+p_{jDe}+p_{jSe}+D_{ie}
           &&(i\in\mathcal U_j,\ 0\le e\le E),\nonumber\\
 &\beta_{je}\le p_{jHe}+p_{jDe}+p_{jSe}
           &&(j\text{ terminal},\ 0\le e\le E),\nonumber\\
 &\beta_{jE}\le p_{jHE}+p_{jDE}
           &&(j\text{ terminal}).
 \label{eq:native_backing}
\end{align}
Terminal backing implies the corresponding user rows because $D_{ie}\ge0$;
their exact elimination changes no feasible point. KV appends use original
object identities, complete prefix operands and these same persistence rules.
The native certified family adds no recomputation columns. A separate
recomputation extension must charge repeat work, gate it on prior production,
restore tier state without a second birth/completion, and explicitly identify
which backing obligations can be discharged by recomputation.

\paragraph{Selected numerical representation.}
A raw FP8 parameter and its converted BF16 parameter are different objects.
For converted object $j$, raw owner $r(j)$, and consumer $i$, define
$R_{ij}=\sum_{k\ni i:\,k\ {\rm reads}\ r(j)}y_k$.
Selected-reader branches replace the corresponding nonterminal user row by
\begin{align}
 \beta_{je}&\le\sum_\tau p_{j\tau e}+D_{ie}+R_{ij},&
 R_{ij}&\le\sum_\tau p_{r(j),\tau e}+D_{ie}.
 \label{eq:native_formats}
\end{align}
The actual selected implementation still requires readiness in its declared
input tier. Original conversion nodes remain covered. Earlier branches retain
their original backing rows; Eq.~\eqref{eq:native_formats} is an identified
alternative contract, not an equivalence transformation of them.

\subsection{Capacities, Staging and Installed Hardware}
Define the transient ordinary occupancy
\[
 o_{j\tau e}=p_{j\tau e}-v_{j\tau e}
              +\sum_{\sigma:\sigma\to\tau}q_{j,\sigma\to\tau,e}.
\]
Let $N=h_{b,\rm arrays}$, $S=h_{b,\rm SRAM}$ in MiB, and $V=h_{b,\rm vector}$.
The source introduces $\chi_e\approx N A_e$ with the four linear inequalities
\begin{align}
 n_-A_e\le\chi_e\le n_+A_e,\qquad
 N+n_+A_e-n_+\lambda_b\le\chi_e
 \le N+n_-A_e-n_-\lambda_b .
 \label{eq:native_workspace}
\end{align}
Let $W_N,W_V$ be the original per-array and active-vector workspace constants,
$P_k$ private panel SRAM, $D_k$ private DRAM workspace, and
$O_{k\tau}=\sum_{j\in O_k:\tau_k=\tau}m_j$ output reservation.
The newest native branch uses
\begin{align}
 &\sum_jm_jo_{jSe}+W_N\chi_e+W_VA_e
        +\sum_k(P_k+O_{kS})a_{ke}
        +(C_{\rm stage}+C_{\rm receiver})\lambda_b
       \le 2^{20}S,\nonumber\\
 &\sum_jm_jo_{jDe}+\sum_k(D_k+O_{kD})a_{ke}
        +\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_{\rm stage}}m_j\delta_{je}
       \le C_D\lambda_b,\nonumber\\
 &\sum_jm_jo_{jHe}+\sum_k O_{kH}a_{ke}\le C_H\lambda_b .
 \label{eq:native_capacity}
\end{align}
Branch-specific absent terms are zero. Entry and exit capacity rows count the
resident state and installed dedicated reservations, without active work or
in-flight transfers. Installed bank, register, controller and receiver
requirements also give affine hardware rows, alongside area and power
proxies. Host/local channel counts and receiver variants are whole-program
choices; arrays, vector lanes, SRAM and DRAM bandwidth remain shared continuous
coordinates inside each branch.

\paragraph{Explicit parameter staging.}
For each stageable owner $j$, the additional coordinates obey
\begin{align}
 &\eta_{j0}=\eta_{jE}=0,\quad
   \eta_{j,e+1}=\eta_{je}+\rho_{je}-\delta_{je},\nonumber\\
 &0\le\delta_{je}\le\eta_{je}\le\beta_{je},\qquad
   0\le\rho_{je}\le p_{jHe}-v_{jHe},\nonumber\\
 &\sum_jm_j\eta_{je}\le C_{\rm stage}\lambda_b,\nonumber\\
 &\sum_jm_j(\eta_{je}-\delta_{je}+\rho_{je})
   +\sum_{j,\ H\in\{\tau,\tau'\}}\min(m_j,C_{\rm stage})q_{j,\tau\to\tau',e}
   +\sum_k R_k^{\rm stage}a_{ke}\le C_{\rm stage}\lambda_b .
 \label{eq:native_staging}
\end{align}
The state bound applies through exit; the transition row applies to action
events. $R_k^{\rm stage}$ is the original implementation's reservation:
ordinary host service uses $\min(B_k^H,C_{\rm stage})$, while full-K and receiver
implementations use their explicit panel/buffer reservation.
The drain enters DRAM state and transient capacity in
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:native_state}--\eqref{eq:native_capacity}.
Dedicated staging and receiver SRAM cannot simultaneously hold ordinary data.

\subsection{Native Resource Demands and Linear Service Envelopes}
For original stream bytes $B_k$, MAC demand $M_k$, and vector work $V_k$,
the native variable-rate demands are
\begin{align}
 Q_e^B&=\sum_{j,\tau\to\tau'}m_jq_{j,\tau\to\tau',e}
          +\sum_k B_kw_{ke}+\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_{\rm stage}}m_j\delta_{je},
          \nonumber\\
 Q_e^N&=\sum_k (M_k/256)w_{ke},\qquad
 Q_e^V=\sum_k V_kw_{ke}.
 \label{eq:native_demands}
\end{align}
Here one native array has $16\times16$ MACs. All traffic on the same DRAM
service pool enters $Q_e^B$ before applying its epigraph. Different resource
services each satisfy $Z_e^r\le d_e$.
For a rate $r\in[\ell,h]$, $u_-=1/h$, $u_+=1/\ell$, the ideal homogenized
reciprocal and product rows are
\begin{align}
 &\ell\lambda_b\le r\le h\lambda_b,\quad
 u_-\lambda_b\le u\le u_+\lambda_b,\nonumber\\
 &u\ge2\lambda_b/a-r/a^2\quad(a\in\mathcal A),\qquad
 u\le((\ell+h)\lambda_b-r)/(\ell h),\nonumber\\
 &0\le Q\le\bar Q\lambda_b,\quad Z\ge0,\quad Z\ge u_-Q,\nonumber\\
 &Z\ge\bar Q u+u_+Q-\bar Q u_+\lambda_b,\quad
 Z\le u_+Q,\quad Z\le\bar Q u+u_-Q-\bar Q u_-\lambda_b .
 \label{eq:native_envelopes}
\end{align}
The implemented LP uses outward-rounded reciprocal endpoints and row
coefficients derived from exact rational interpretations of supplied binary64
values. Each coefficient error is paid against its original coordinate box in
the row RHS, rounded upward; errors on unbounded coordinates are refused.
Thus stored rows contain the physical graph without asserting equality to it.
The source demand bounds $\bar Q$ cover the full declared generator domain,
not just activated working columns.

Fixed-rate ports use integer-scaled service rows. With $B_k^H,B_k^L$ the
original host/local byte coefficients, $t_k^{\rm setup}$ host setup ticks,
$B_k^{\rm stage}$ the staging service bytes and $t_k^{\rm recv}$ receiver ticks,
\begin{align}
 &\sum_{j,\ H\in\{\tau,\tau'\}}m_jq_{j,\tau\to\tau',e}
       +\sum_jm_j\rho_{je}+\sum_k(B_k^H+r_Ht_k^{\rm setup})w_{ke}
       \le r_Hd_e,\nonumber\\
 &\sum_{j,\ S\in\{\tau,\tau'\}}m_jq_{j,\tau\to\tau',e}
       +\sum_k B_k^Lw_{ke}\le r_Ld_e,\nonumber\\
 &\sum_jm_j(\rho_{je}+\delta_{je})
       +2\sum_{j,\ H\in\{\tau,\tau'\}}m_jq_{j,\tau\to\tau',e}
       +\sum_k B_k^{\rm stage}w_{ke}\le r_{\rm stage}d_e,\nonumber\\
 &\sum_k t_k^{\rm recv}w_{ke}\le d_e,\qquad
   \sum_k t_k^{\min}w_{ke}\le d_e .
 \label{eq:native_fixed_ports}
\end{align}
Stage-owner sums are restricted to $\mathcal J_{\rm stage}$.
Ordinary kernels pay $B_k^{\rm stage}=2B_k^H$; streamed implementations
substitute their paid staging recipe. Absent receiver/setup/floor terms are zero.
The current source retains the floor row when its implementation contract
requires it. The objective is $\kappa\sum_e d_e$, with $\kappa$ the exact
stored binary64 conversion from ns to ms. Integral replay adds its declared
clock, descriptor and dispatch accounting; LP event sharing alone does not
establish realizable kernel overlap.

\subsection{Direct Roofline and Physical Constraints}
\label{app:physical_rooflines}
The strengthened fixed-chip implementation uses one installed chip $\bar h_b$
per whole-program branch. Its scaled coordinates satisfy
$h_b=\lambda_b\bar h_b$; no operation chooses a separate chip. Let
$\bar C_b$ be installed BF16 matrix throughput in FLOP/ns, and
$\bar B_{br}$ the delivered byte/ns rate of interface $r$.
With original useful work $F_k^{\rm useful}\le F_k^{\rm issued}$ and linear
traffic incidence $\mathsf B_{bre}$, the source adds
\begin{align}
 &\sum_k F_k^{\rm issued}w_{ke}\le\bar C_b d_e,
 &\mathsf B_{bre}(q,w,\rho,\delta)&\le\bar B_{br}d_e,\nonumber\\
 &\sum_k V_kw_{ke}\le\bar V_b d_e,
 &b&\in\mathcal B_{\rm phys},\ e\in\mathcal E_b .
 \label{eq:hard_roofline_rows}
\end{align}
For the suite's $16\times16$ arrays at 1 GHz,
$\bar C_b=512\bar N_b$ and
$F_k^{\rm issued}=512\,\mathrm{jobs}_k\,\mathrm{bundle}_k\,
\mathrm{reductionCycles}_k$; one MAC is two FLOPs. The demand sums include
every concurrently active implementation and transfer sharing a resource.
HBM, host, local SRAM, DMA staging and aggregate bank ports have separate
incidences and limits. The suite's external-copy staging row charges at least
$64\lceil m/32\rceil$ bytes for a transfer of $m$ bytes, accounting for its
32-byte-word write and read. This is an alignment-independent necessary bound;
replay pays actual alignment, commands and queue costs. Kernel local traffic,
SRAM-copy endpoints and DMA staging enter one aggregate bank-port row.

For a selected branch ($\lambda_b=1$), summing over the same event interval
with total duration $T=\sum_e d_e>0$ gives
\begin{equation}
 \mathrm{MFU}=\frac{\sum_{e,k}F_k^{\rm useful}w_{ke}}{\bar C_bT}
 \le\frac{\sum_{e,k}F_k^{\rm issued}w_{ke}}{\bar C_bT}\le1,
 \qquad
 \mathrm{MBU}_r=\frac{\sum_e\mathsf B_{bre}}{\bar B_{br}T}\le1.
 \label{eq:hard_roofline_guarantee}
\end{equation}
Traffic includes the represented reads, writes, repeated loads and transfers;
vector arithmetic is not counted as BF16 matrix work. No tolerance declares a
positive resource residual zero at exact admission. These inequalities remain
linear because the installed rates are branch constants. In a variable-hardware
relaxation, $C(h)d_e$ is bilinear; its McCormick envelope does not provide this
exact fixed-chip guarantee. Fractional branch mixtures and intermediate solver
iterates are not reported as fabricated or executable chips.

Write installed-component area and all-components-active peak power as
$\mathsf A_b^\lambda=a_{b0}\lambda_b+a_b^Th_b$ and
$\mathsf P_b^\lambda=p_{b0}\lambda_b+p_b^Th_b$. The shared constraint compiler
adds the following exact affine rows:
\begin{align}
 &\mathsf A_b^\lambda\le600\lambda_b,
 \quad\mathsf P_b^\lambda\le300\lambda_b,
 \quad\mathsf P_b^\lambda\le2\mathsf A_b^\lambda,\nonumber\\
 &\frac{h_{b,B}}{\eta_H\beta_H}\le n_H^{\max}\lambda_b,
 \quad\frac{h_{b,B}}{\eta_H\beta_H}
       +\frac{\bar B_{b,\rm host}}{\eta_{\rm host}\beta_{\rm host}}\lambda_b
       \le n_{\rm pkg}^{\max}\lambda_b,\nonumber\\
 &h_{b,B}\le\bar B_{b,\rm controller}\lambda_b,
 \quad 2^{20}h_{b,S}=65536\,n_b\lambda_b,
 \quad n_bh_{b,N}\le5{,}000{,}000\lambda_b .
 \label{eq:physical_budget_rows}
\end{align}
Here area is in $\mathrm{mm}^2$, power in W, $\beta$ is byte/ns per signal pin,
$\eta$ is delivered efficiency, $n_b$ is the fixed installed bank count,
and $h_{b,B},h_{b,S},h_{b,N}$ denote bandwidth, SRAM MiB and arrays.
The declared BF16 scenario uses at most 4,096 combined host/HBM signal pins,
whole 64-KiB banks with $n_b\le4{,}096$, bank-array aspect between $1/4$ and 4,
and a logic-depth proxy at most 35. Each bank supplies a combined 32-byte/cycle
port at 1 GHz. Installed-buffer lower bounds prevent hardware arrays, vector
registers or DMA storage from borrowing memory already assigned to live data.
Bank organization, pipeline depth and interface choices are fixed template
constants; an invalid template cannot become an admitted positive-weight branch.

Peak power uses 1.2 mW per BF16 MAC in the revised analytical scenario, with
separately priced SRAM, vector, controller and interface components. It is not
multiplied by MFU. Inherited area coefficients and their sensitivity factors
remain declared assumptions. The NoC-size and chip-average density rows are
proxies, not detailed network congestion or hotspot models; arbitrary bank/link
overlap additionally needs its actual resource incidence. LP construction and
recovery use the same identified coefficient set and gates.

\paragraph{Implementation and certificate scope.}
These rows are compiled by \texttt{native\_physical\_contract.py} and
\texttt{native\_suite\_physical\_rows.py}; the fixed-rate native wrapper also
adds direct compute, bandwidth and vector ceilings. The complete saved
Llama-13B execution passes 520,216,367 added scalar rows and four hardware
boxes. Integration into every other master construction path is unfinished.
The 21-branch $0.0850\%$ certificate in
Appendix~\ref{app:native_lp_certificate} retains its original rows and costs;
it is not a certificate for the strengthened physical family.
